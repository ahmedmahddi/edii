Verdict

Worth testing on real people, but only with one segment

The product sells to people who already lose evenings rebuilding a register before an audit: certified industrial sites, multi-site groups, consultants and foreign-owned exporters. Small owner-run sites, hotels and public enterprises will not buy software on their own, whatever the price. Almost every BUY came with a condition (a trial, a formal quote, an export, a contract clause, a consultant who switches first), so expect real conversion well below the panel's.

First test, this week: pick 10 existing ABC clients who are ISO 14001 or 45001 certified. Load each one's register into the app before the call. Offer a 3-month paid pilot at 600 TND, credited against the first year. Success means at least 3 of the 10 pay the invoice within two weeks.
